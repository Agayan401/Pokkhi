Sources

Pokkhi is the result of more than six years of documenting and compiling information on the birds of Assam. The directory has been developed by carefully bringing together information from trusted published references, scientific resources, field observations and valuable knowledge shared by local communities. Every effort has been made to cross-reference information from multiple sources while preserving the diversity of indigenous and local bird names used across Assam.

Field Documentation & Community Knowledge

A significant part of Pokkhi has been shaped by years of field observations and interactions with birdwatchers, photographers and local communities across Assam. Indigenous and local bird names often vary between districts, languages and communities. Wherever possible, these names have been documented and verified through field documentation, discussions with knowledgeable individuals and approved community contributions.

Disclaimer

Bird taxonomy, conservation status, distribution and nomenclature evolve over time. Although every effort is made to keep Pokkhi current, users are encouraged to consult additional reliable sources when making important identification or conservation decisions.

Privacy Policy

Pokkhi respects your privacy. Information submitted through the "Suggest a Bird" form—including bird names, indigenous or local names, location, notes and contributor name—is used solely to review submissions, improve the directory and acknowledge approved contributions. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Pokkhi uses Google Analytics to better understand how visitors use the website. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage information such as pages visited, browser and device type, approximate geographic region and general interaction with the website. This information helps us improve the content, usability and overall experience of Pokkhi. No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected through Google Analytics.

By using this website, you consent to the collection of anonymous usage data through Google Analytics for the purposes described above. If you submit information through the "Suggest a Bird" form, you acknowledge that approved contributions and the contributor's name may be displayed publicly on Pokkhi.
